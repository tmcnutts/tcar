About Twin Cities Animal Rescue

TCAR provides emergency foster care and medical support to animals in need throughout the Twin Cities metro. We partner with clinics and shelters to rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome pets with loving families.

Our Story

Founded in 2014 by a group of local volunteers and veterinary professionals, TCAR began as a small network of fosters helping animals in crisis. Since then we've grown into a coordinated rescue organization that focuses on medical intervention, short-term fostering, and community education.

Core Values

These guiding principles inform every decision we make at TCAR.

Compassion-first Care

We prioritize the comfort and welfare of each animal, ensuring pain is managed and behavior needs are addressed before rehoming.

Medical Excellence

Our network of partner veterinarians provides advanced diagnostics, surgeries, and follow-up care so every animal receives appropriate treatment.

Community Partnership

We collaborate with shelters, clinics, and volunteers to maximize capacity and keep animals safe in the region.

Transparency & Education

We share outcomes, costs, and best practices with the community to build trust and empower responsible pet guardianship.
