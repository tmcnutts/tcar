What can you do?

Short, actionable ways to support TCAR.

Hands-on

  • Adopt: Complete our adoption form and visit weekend events.
  • Foster: Short-term medical and behavior fosters (2–8 weeks). We provide supplies.
  • Volunteer: Transport, animal care, or event shifts (2–4 hours).

Support & Outreach

  • Donate: One-time or monthly gifts for medical care and supplies.
  • Supply Drives: Drop off food, blankets, leashes at intake hours.
  • Share: Post adoptable pets and tag @TCAR to amplify reach.
