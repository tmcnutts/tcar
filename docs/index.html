We accept animals that need saving and get them ready for their forever home.

Impact Statements

  • In the last year, TCAR provided emergency foster care to 142 stray, injured, and neglected animals, helping each one regain health and trust.
  • Our team successfully placed 89 dogs, 57 cats, and 23 small animals into permanent homes, creating brighter futures for pets and families alike.
  • Through community outreach and medical care, we treated 310 animals for illness, injury, and vaccinations, reducing suffering and preventing overcrowding in local shelters.
  • Every donation and volunteer hour helped rescue a new life—our foster network alone supported 174 animals while they waited for adoption.

How You Can Help

Choose what works best for you — here are short, specific ways to help right now.

Hands-on Help

  • Adopt: Complete our adoption form and meet animals at weekend adoption events (Saturdays, 10–2).
  • Foster: Short-term (2–8 weeks) medical or behavioral fosters needed — we provide supplies and vet coordination.
  • Volunteer: Transport to vet appointments, kennel cleaning, or event staffing (shifts are 2–4 hours).

Support & Outreach

  • Donate: One-time or monthly gifts help cover spay/neuter and emergency care; send via the Contact page.
  • Supply Drives: Drop off unopened food, leashes, collars, and blankets at our intake center during open hours.
  • Share: Post adoptable animal profiles and event flyers on social media — tag @TCAR to amplify reach.

Questions or ready to help? Contact us or see Services We Offer for details.
