We accept animals that need saving and get them ready for their forever home.

Impact Statements

  • In the last year, TCAR provided emergency foster care to 142 stray, injured, and neglected animals, helping each one regain health and trust.
  • Our team successfully placed 89 dogs, 57 cats, and 23 small animals into permanent homes, creating brighter futures for pets and families alike.
  • Through community outreach and medical care, we treated 310 animals for illness, injury, and vaccinations, reducing suffering and preventing overcrowding in local shelters.
  • Every donation and volunteer hour helped rescue a new life—our foster network alone supported 174 animals while they waited for adoption.

How You Can Help

There are many simple ways to help animals in need:

  • Adopt a rescued pet and give them a forever home.
  • Foster an animal while they recover and prepare for adoption.
  • Volunteer your time to support animal care, events, and outreach.
  • Donate money, supplies, or medical support to help rescue animals.
  • Share our mission and animals in need with friends and family.
